FAQ

Common Questions About Window Repair

How do I know if my windows need repair or replacement?

Look for signs like drafts near windows, condensation or fog between panes, difficulty opening or closing, visible frame rot, or noticeably higher heating bills. If your windows are over 15-20 years old, they may lack modern energy-efficient features. Keyprime offers free inspections — we'll assess every window and give honest recommendations on whether repair or replacement makes the most sense for your situation and budget.

What's the difference between double-pane and triple-pane windows?

Double-pane windows have two layers of glass with a gas fill between them, providing good insulation for most climates. Triple-pane windows add a third layer of glass and an additional gas fill chamber, offering superior insulation, noise reduction, and condensation resistance. For Minnesota's extreme winters, triple-pane windows can significantly reduce heating costs and eliminate cold spots near windows.

How long does a window replacement take?

Most standard window replacements take 30-60 minutes per window. A typical home with 8-12 windows can usually be completed in one to two days. Custom or specialty windows may require additional time. We schedule installations to minimize disruption and always clean up thoroughly when we're done.

What does the True Lifetime Warranty cover?

Our True Lifetime Warranty covers installation, workmanship, inspections, and quality control for as long as you own your home. This goes beyond the standard manufacturer warranty that most contractors offer. If anything goes wrong with our installation, we'll come back and make it right — no questions asked, no fine print, no expiration date.

Can you fix foggy or condensation-filled windows?

Foggy windows indicate a broken seal between the glass panes. In some cases, we can replace just the insulated glass unit (IGU) without replacing the entire window frame, which saves you money. During your free inspection, we'll determine whether a seal repair, glass-only replacement, or full window replacement is the best solution.
