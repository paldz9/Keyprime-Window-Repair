The Problem

Is Cold Air Sneaking
Into Your Home?

When Minnesota temperatures plunge below zero, even small window gaps become big problems. Here's what drafty windows are really costing you:

Skyrocketing Energy Bills

Drafty windows can increase heating costs by 25–30%, costing hundreds of dollars each winter season.

Cold Spots & Discomfort

Rooms near windows feel noticeably colder, forcing your furnace to work overtime without ever feeling truly warm.

Condensation & Ice Buildup

Moisture between panes causes fog, frost, and even ice — damaging frames and promoting mold growth.

Decreasing Home Value

Old, inefficient windows are one of the first things home inspectors flag — and buyers notice.
