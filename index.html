The Problem

Is Cold Air Sneaking Into Your Home?

When Minnesota temperatures plunge below zero, even small window gaps become big problems. Here's what drafty windows are really costing you:

Skyrocketing Energy Bills

Drafty windows can increase heating costs by 25–30%, costing hundreds of dollars each winter season.

Cold Spots & Discomfort

Rooms near windows feel noticeably colder, forcing your furnace to work overtime without ever feeling truly warm.

Condensation & Ice Buildup

Moisture between panes causes fog, frost, and even ice — damaging frames and promoting mold growth.

Decreasing Home Value

Old, inefficient windows are one of the first things home inspectors flag — and buyers notice.

Engineering & Technology

U-PVC Vinyl

High-strength, un-plasticized poly-vinyl chloride — the same maintenance-free material used in medical applications. Wipes clean with soap and water, never needs painting or staining.

Multiple Hollow Chamber Design

Carefully engineered multiple hollow chambers create layers of "dead" air within the frame, providing superior thermal insulation and structural rigidity.

Intercept Insulating Spacer

The Intercept spacer system uses a one-piece U-channel design that outperforms conventional box spacers, reducing condensation and heat transfer at the glass edge.

Low-E Coated Glass

Low-E glass blocks up to 95% of the sun's damaging ultraviolet rays while allowing natural light in, protecting your furniture, carpets, and artwork from fading.

R-5 Green Windows

Triple pane glass with Duralite® spacers achieves an R-5 Green rating — improving thermal performance by up to 50% over standard double pane, reducing energy costs year-round.
