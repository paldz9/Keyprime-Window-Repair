FAQ

Common Questions About Roofing

What should I do if I have a leaky roof?

If your ceiling is cracked, sagging, or has water stains on it, you likely have a leaky roof. You should act quickly to move things out of the way, contain the water, and alleviate the water pressure if your ceiling is sagging. Then, call a roofing contractor like Keyprime to get a professional opinion regarding short-term and long-term solutions. Ultimately, you'll need to locate the leak, and get your roof repaired. If the leak is related to an insurance claim, Keyprime can assist with the claim process.

Why are asphalt shingles so popular for roofs in Minnesota?

Asphalt shingles are the most affordable roofing material available, and the least expensive material to install. They also come in a variety of colors to match any home. Asphalt shingles are relatively durable and can last up to 50 years with proper maintenance and inspections. For best results, asphalt shingles should be installed by a roofing contractor.

Why are metal roofs a good choice for Minnesota homes?

With a lifespan of up to 70 years or more, today's metal roofing options offer homeowners a lifetime of worry-free roofing, even in a tough Midwestern climate. Their fireproof quality can lower insurance costs, and their contemporary look is a beautiful way to modernize your home.

Why should I have my roofing contractor work directly with my insurance provider?

Keyprime not only inspects roofs for storm damage, but can also work directly with your insurance provider to get the damage repaired quickly with no upfront cost to you. Keyprime employs a team of experienced professionals that specialize in working through insurance claims to make sure they are processed properly and on time.
