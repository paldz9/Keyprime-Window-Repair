We don't just install windows — we stand behind every one. Keyprime's True Lifetime Warranty covers our workmanship for the entire manufacturer-specified lifetime of your products.

Why It Matters

What Makes Our Warranty Different

Most contractors' warranties only cover the product itself — meaning if anything goes wrong with the installation, you're on your own. Keyprime's True Lifetime Warranty goes further.

Labor & Workmanship Covered

Our warranty covers defects caused by faulty workmanship due to noncompliance with the building code in force at the time of installation. If we made the mistake, we fix it — period.

Lasts the Product's Lifetime

The warranty spans the manufacturer's specified lifetime of the materials we install — asphalt shingles, siding, gutters, windows, and doors. It lasts as long as your products do.

Code-Compliant Installation

Every installation meets or exceeds building codes and manufacturer specifications — proper flashing, sealing, insulation, and nailing patterns. Documented and inspected.

GAF Master Elite Certified

As a GAF Master Elite contractor, Keyprime meets the highest standards in the roofing industry. Only 2% of roofing contractors qualify — enabling our strongest warranty offerings.

Products Covered

What the Warranty Covers

The True Lifetime Warranty applies to all materials installed by Keyprime, exclusively covering labor and workmanship.

Windows

All window installations — vinyl, wood, clad, single-hung, double-hung, casement, bay, bow, and specialty windows.

Siding

Vinyl siding, fiber cement, and all other siding materials installed by Keyprime crews.

Roofing

Asphalt shingle installations backed by both our workmanship warranty and GAF's manufacturer warranties.

Gutters & Doors

Gutter systems, gutter guards, entry doors, and patio doors — all installation workmanship covered.

Important: Product or material defects are subject to the manufacturer's warranty, which may or may not apply separately. The Keyprime warranty exclusively covers labor and workmanship.

Terms & Conditions

Warranty Details

Coverage

KeyPrime Roofing and Remodeling warrants that for the period spanning the manufacturer's specified lifetime of asphalt shingles, siding, gutters, windows, and doors installed by KeyPrime, its work will be free from defects caused by faulty workmanship due to noncompliance with the building code in force at the time of installation.

What's Included

  • Installation labor on all materials listed above
  • Defects caused by faulty workmanship
  • Noncompliance with building codes at the time of installation

Claim Requirements

  • All warranty claims must be made within 90 days after discovery of the issue
  • If a claim is not reported within 90 days, customer's rights under the warranty are waived
  • Claims not covered under this warranty may incur a $150 service charge

Transferability

This warranty is not transferable. It applies exclusively to the original customer who contracted with Keyprime for the installation. The warranty is a part of the parties' agreement and is incorporated by reference.

What's Not Covered

Warranty Exclusions

Transparency matters. Here's what falls outside our workmanship warranty so there are no surprises.

01

Late Reporting

Loss or damage not reported within 90 days after discovery, unless KeyPrime had actual notice.

02

Customer-Supplied Materials

Defects in design, installation, or materials which the owner supplied, installed, or directed to be installed.

03

Secondary Damages

Secondary loss or damage such as personal injury or property damage resulting from a covered issue.

04

Normal Wear & Tear

Loss or damage from normal wear, normal shrinkage from drying within building standard tolerances.

05

Ventilation & Moisture

Dampness and condensation due to insufficient ventilation in the structure.

06

Third-Party Alterations

Negligence, improper maintenance, or alteration of the work by anyone other than KeyPrime.

07

Landscaping & Insects

Landscaping or insect loss or damage is not covered under this warranty.

08

Mold & Biological Growth

Loss from failure to maintain the work in good repair, including mold, fungus, bacteria, or other biological organisms.

09

Failure to Mitigate

Damage which the customer, whenever feasible, has not taken timely action to minimize.

10

Non-Residential Use

Damage occurring after the structure is no longer used primarily as a residence.

11

Acts of God

Fire, explosion, smoke, water escape, windstorm, hail, lightning, falling trees, aircraft, flood, and earthquake — unless caused by failure to comply with building standards.

12

Soil & Pre-Existing Conditions

Soil movement compensated by legislation or insurance, soil conditions on customer-owned land, and defects in the existing structure not caused by our work.

Filing a Claim

How to File a Warranty Claim

If you believe you have a warranty issue, follow these steps to get it resolved quickly.

1

Contact Us

Call us at (952) 522-3705 or fill out the form on our contact page. Describe the issue and when you discovered it.

2

Document the Issue

Take photos or videos of the damage. Note the date you first discovered the problem — remember, claims must be filed within 90 days.

3

Inspection & Resolution

Our team will inspect the issue. If it's a covered workmanship defect, we'll repair it at no cost. If it falls outside the warranty, we'll explain why and discuss options.

Whether you need to file a claim or simply want to learn more about your coverage, we're here to help.
